# teshuvance 🔄

*The act of turning the whole self back toward GoD — not the feeling of guilt, not the backward grief of remorse, but the positive directional turn the Prophets call for: shuv, turn.*

teshuvance is built on the Hebrew **teshuvah** (תְּשׁוּבָה), from the root **shuv** (שׁוּב), to turn, to return — the most frequent prophetic call in the Hebrew scriptures, the turning-back of the Days of Teshuvah — welded to the act-quality suffix **-ance**, so it names the *act* of turning, not the state of having turned. Greek **metanoia** (μετάνοια, *meta* beyond + *nous* mind) and Arabic **tawbah** (توبة, from *taba*, to return) confirm the same directional theology from inside their own tongues; the Syriac monks knew it as continuous, lifelong reorientation.

teshuvance names the positive worship-act of turning, set carefully apart from guilt (a feeling that may never turn), remorse (backward-looking, without movement), and contrition (sorrow without the act). Its light is that it is the most hopeful word the cathedral owns — the door is not locked from the inside: as the creature turns (*tawbah*), the divine Al-Tawwāb turns toward the creature, and relationship is restored. Its shadow is the turn that never turns — the insincere gesture that says *shuv* while the feet keep their old direction — or the call to turn wielded as a lash instead of an open door. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.32/10**.
